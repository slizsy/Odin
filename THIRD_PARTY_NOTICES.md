# third party notices

odin's built-in username site list (`src/modules/user/sites_data.c`, generated by
`tools/gen_sites.py`) is adapted from the site data of these two projects. thanks to everyone
who maintains them, keeping hundreds of site rules working is a lot of work.

- **maigret**: https://github.com/soxoj/maigret
- **sherlock**: https://github.com/sherlock-project/sherlock

both are MIT licensed, their license texts are below.

**whatsmyname** (https://github.com/WebBreacher/WhatsMyName) is not bundled. its data is
CC BY-SA 4.0, so odin only reads a `wmn-data.json` you download yourself
(`odin user --sites wmn-data.json johndoe`).

the `odin phone` validator uses metadata from **libphonenumber**
(https://github.com/google/libphonenumber, Apache-2.0), turned into a compact C table by
`tools/gen_phone.py` (`src/modules/phone/phone_data.c`). the Apache-2.0 license text is below.

the `odin email` disposable-domain list (`src/modules/email/disposable_data.c`, generated by
`tools/gen_disposable.py`) comes from **disposable-email-domains**
(https://github.com/disposable-email-domains/disposable-email-domains), released under CC0.

`odin domain` and `odin ip` don't bundle any third-party data, they query public services at
runtime: dns-over-https (cloudflare / google), RDAP registries, crt.sh certificate
transparency, ipwho.is and gravatar.
